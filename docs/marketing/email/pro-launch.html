Quick note from Grant, the rider and developer behind Setups.Bike. Thanks for keeping your tire pressure and suspension settings in the app.

Setups.Bike now has a Pro plan, with three things I kept wanting on my own bikes:

  • Saved setups per bike. Keep "Dry", "Wet" and "Bike park" (or whatever you ride) side by side, and switch in a tap instead of re-entering clicks.
  • Ride notes. Log the trail, conditions and how it rode, with a snapshot of that day's settings, so you can see what worked last time.
  • Share a setup. Send a link to a riding buddy, group chat or forum. Anyone can view it and copy it into their own garage.

Pro also lifts the bike limit, so the whole quiver fits. It's $2.99 a month or $24 a year, and you can cancel any time.

Free isn't going anywhere. Free still saves tire pressure, fork and shock settings, clicks and tokens for up to 3 bikes, with no card needed. If you already have more than 3 bikes, you keep every one of them. You just can't add another on Free.
