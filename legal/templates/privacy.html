{{appName}} ("we", "us") is a small app for recording bike suspension and tire settings, run by {{operatorName}}. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and what you can ask us to do with your data. Questions: {{supportEmail}}.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture link. We store your name and email in our database. Your profile picture is shown from Google's servers and is only kept in your own browser.
  • What you enter: your bikes (brand and model), their tire and suspension settings, their order in your garage, and, if you use Pro features, saved setups, ride notes, optional rider details such as weight, and setups you choose to share.
  • If you subscribe to Pro: your plan, its status and renewal date, and the customer and subscription IDs that Stripe gives us. We never see or store your card details. Stripe collects and holds them.
  • Technical logs: when the app's servers handle a request they write a log, which can include your user ID and the time. We use these only to keep the app working and to fix problems, and keep them for up to {{logRetentionDays}} days.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your garage tied to your account.
  • To save and show your bikes and settings.
  • To run Pro subscriptions: billing, renewals, receipts and failed-payment notices.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to answer you when you contact us.

Shared setups

If you share a setup, anyone with the link can see that setup's bike and settings without signing in. It doesn't show your email. You can revoke a link at any time, after which it stops working.

Who helps us run the app

These services process data for us, only as needed to provide the app:

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting the app and its servers, and storing logs (United States).
  • Neon: our database (United States).
  • Cloudflare: delivering the website securely.
  • Stripe: payments for Pro. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your browser

To keep you signed in between visits, the app stores a sign-in token and your basic profile in your browser's local storage. Logging out removes them. The token expires after 7 days. Google's sign-in and Stripe's checkout pages may set their own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and garage data for as long as you have an account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and garage data. Server logs are deleted automatically after {{logRetentionDays}} days. Stripe keeps payment records as its own legal obligations require.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete your bikes in the app at any time. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account, email {{supportEmail}} from the address you sign in with, and we'll respond within 30 days. Security
